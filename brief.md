##Overview

This brief explores six important areas involved in building a secure government single sign-on service. Citizens should be able to use one account to access government services through a mobile app and website. The service must protect personal information, verify users securely, and remain reliable. Software standards help explain how login and delegated access work, while artificial intelligence can support the detection of suspicious login attempts. Mobile permissions, secure networking, phishing-resistant authentication, and data protection rules also affect the service. These areas are connected because a weakness in one area may affect the security and trustworthiness of the whole system. This research reviews practical guidance and official resources to understand the main requirements before the service is launched.
